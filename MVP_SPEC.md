# MVP Spec

Stack: Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind, Supabase.

V1: flujo completo mock sin login, sin pagos integrados y sin OCR real.

Rutas:

- `/`
- `/create`
- `/create/review`
- `/create/payment`
- `/create/share`
- `/bill/[shareId]`
- `/bill/[shareId]/join`
- `/bill/[shareId]/pay/[participantId]`

Fases futuras: Supabase, upload imagenes, OCR + IA, asignacion inteligente y resumen compartible.
